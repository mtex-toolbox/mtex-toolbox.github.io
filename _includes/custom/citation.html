{%- comment -%} The visible attribution block at the foot of every generated page, included from _layouts/page.html. This is the part that actually travels. A retrieval-augmented assistant quoting a paragraph of this documentation carries the surrounding text with it, so an attribution written in prose -- naming MTEX, the canonical URL and the paper -- survives the trip in a way that a tag does not. The BibTeX block is here for the same reason: it is the citation in the form a reader (human or machine) can paste without reformatting it. Deliberately in the Jekyll layer rather than in matlab/web.xsl: web.xsl would mean republishing all ~2700 generated pages through MATLAB (hours, plus a large image diff) to change a sentence. Here it is one file and takes effect on the next Pages build. The values come from _config.yml, shared with _includes/custom/metadata.html so the human-readable and machine-readable citation cannot drift apart. {%- endcomment -%}
