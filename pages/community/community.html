Community

MTEX is built by the people who use it

Materials scientists, geologists and mathematicians develop MTEX together with its users: 43 people have contributed code, the forum holds 1,400 discussions, and users have met at the workshop in Freiberg since 2008.

Ask a questionThe forum on GitHub: questions, answers and announcements. Search it first, most questions have been asked before. Report a bugEvery bug report is welcome. A few lines of code and a data set that show the problem help most. Contribute codeNew functions, fixes and interfaces to further file formats, through pull requests on GitHub. Improve the documentationEvery page has an edit link. Corrections, worked examples and explanations are all welcome. Add-onsToolboxes built on MTEX, from slip transmission to finite element meshes. Share a scriptHelper functions and analysis scripts from other users, and how to share your own. WorkshopTwo weeks in Freiberg with the developers and other users. The next one is in March 2027. VideosRecorded lectures and exercises from past workshops. The teamWho develops MTEX, and who has contributed. Cite MTEXThe papers behind MTEX and its algorithms, and which one to cite for what. ContactQuestions that do not belong in the forum go to the developers directly.
