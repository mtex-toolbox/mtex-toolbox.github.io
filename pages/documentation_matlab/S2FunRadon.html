The Spherical Radon Transform

The spherical Radon transform \(\mathcal R\) replaces the value of a function at a point by its mean value along the great circle perpendicular to that point,

\[ \mathcal R f(\vec x) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\vec v \perp \vec x} f(\vec v)\,\mathrm{d}\vec v. \]

It is the spherical analogue of the classical Radon transform behind computer tomography, and in texture analysis it is the operation that turns an ODF into a pole figure. This page treats it for ordinary spherical functions; see calcPDF for the orientation space version.
