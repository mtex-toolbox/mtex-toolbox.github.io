evaluate a spherical vector field given on a triangulation

The values at the vertices are interpolated linearly by barycentric coordinates; for an antipodal field the axis is the leading eigenvector of the interpolated outer products.

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} v = eval(sVF,nodes) {% endhighlight %}

The field at the directions nodes.

Input

sVF S2VectorFieldTri
nodes interpolation nodes vector3d

Output

v vector3d
