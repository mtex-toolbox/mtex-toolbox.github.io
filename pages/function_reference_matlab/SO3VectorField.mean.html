mean of a vector field on SO(3)

The vector field is averaged over an equispaced grid of orientations.

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} v = mean(SO3VF) {% endhighlight %}

Over a grid of resolution 2.5 degree.

{% highlight matlab %} v = mean(SO3VF,'resolution',1*degree) {% endhighlight %}

Over a finer grid.

Input

SO3VF SO3VectorField

Options

resolution resolution of the grid, default 2.5*degree

Output

v vector3d
