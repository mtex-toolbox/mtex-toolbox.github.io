The Fejer kernel has the unique property of being a convergent finite series in Fourier coefficients with an integral of one. In contrast to Dirichlet kernel the Fejer kernel is nonnegative.

It is defined by its Fourier series

\[ F_n(x) = \frac{1}{n+1} \, \sum\limits_{j=0}^n D_j(x),\]

where \(D_j\) is the j-th Dirichlet kernel.

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} psi = S1FejerKernel(n) {% endhighlight %}

Input

n bandwidth
