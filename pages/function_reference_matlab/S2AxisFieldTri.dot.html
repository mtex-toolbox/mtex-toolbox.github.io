pointwise inner product of an axis field with a field or a direction

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} sF = dot(sVF,sVF2) {% endhighlight %}

The inner product of two fields at every vertex.

{% highlight matlab %} sF = dot(sVF,v) {% endhighlight %}

The inner product with the direction v at every vertex.

Input

sVF S2AxisFieldTri
sVF2 S2AxisField
v vector3d

Output

sF S2FunTri
