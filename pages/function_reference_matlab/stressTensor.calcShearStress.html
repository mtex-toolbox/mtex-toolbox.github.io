shear stress

The resolved shear stress of a stress tensor on the plane with normal m in direction n is the contraction of the stress tensor with the Schmid tensor of m and n. With several planes and directions the largest one is returned together with its plane and direction.

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} [tauMax,m,n,tau] = calcShearStress(sigma,m,n) {% endhighlight %}

The largest shear stress over the pairs (m,n).

{% highlight matlab %} [tauMax,m,n,tau] = calcShearStress(sigma,m,n,'symmetrise') {% endhighlight %}

Over all symmetrically equivalent pairs.

Input

sigma stressTensor
m normal vector the the slip or twinning plane
n Burgers vector (slip) or twin shear direction (twinning)

Options

symmetrise consider also all symmetrically equivalent planes and directions

Output

tauMax maximum shear stress
m active plane
n active direction
tau shear stresses with respect to all planes
