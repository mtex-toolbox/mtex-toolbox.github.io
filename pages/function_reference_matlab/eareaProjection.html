equal area or Schmidt projection

Scales the radius by sqrt(2*(1-cos(theta))), which preserves area and makes it the right projection for judging densities. The MTEX default.

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} sP = eareaProjection(sR) [x,y] = sP.project(v) v = sP.iproject(x,y) {% endhighlight %}

Input

sR sphericalRegion the projection is restricted to
v vector3d
x, y plane coordinates

Class Properties

sR sphericalRegion the projection is restricted to
pC plottingConvention, which direction points east
antipodal identify v and -v
