gbcCustom grain boundary criterion from a custom per-pixel property

Separates two neighbouring measurements by a grain boundary when a user-supplied property differs by more than a threshold. The property may be numeric (scalar per pixel), a vector3d or a quaternion (then the angle between the two is used).

Syntax

{% highlight matlab %} criterion = gbcCustom('custom',myProp,'delta',5*degree); out = criterion.eval(ebsd,i,j); {% endhighlight %}

Output

out = 1 no boundary (property difference below delta > same grain)
out = 0 grain boundary
