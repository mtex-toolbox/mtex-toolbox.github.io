the regularised EM mixture of Botev et al. with ceil(sqrt(n)) Gaussians, fitted on the data
scaled to the unit box, evaluated at the rows of grid; the density and the bandwidth

## Syntax

```python
kdeN(X, grid, gam=None, rng=None)
```
