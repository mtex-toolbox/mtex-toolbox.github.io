the discrepancy of two spherical densities or weighted point sets, D_2, D_cap or L2 (E_L);
the shared form of S2Fun/discrepancy and vector3d/discrepancy, info=True adds the details

## Syntax

```python
sphericalDiscrepancy(a, b, metric='D_2', *, weights=None, weights2=None, bandwidth=None, degreeWeights=None, numCenters=2049, numHeights=257, centers=None, squared=False, info=False)
```
