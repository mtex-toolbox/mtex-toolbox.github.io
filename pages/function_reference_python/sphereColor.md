{% raw %}
the colour of a point of the colour sphere: the radius, 1 at the white centre and 0 at
the black one, and the azimuth as the hue

## Syntax

```python
sphereColor(radius, rho, grayValue=(0.2, 0.5), grayGradient=(0.5, 0.5), colorStretching=1.0, gray=None, hueCorrection=True)
```
