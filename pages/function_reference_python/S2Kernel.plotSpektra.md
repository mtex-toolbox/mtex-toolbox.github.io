plotSpectrum(psi), plotSpectrum(odf): the Chebyshev coefficients of a kernel, or the harmonic
power of a function, per degree (MATLAB's plotSpektra)

## Syntax

```python
plotSpectrum(x, bandwidth=None, logarithmic=False, **kw)
```
