MTEX workshop · Freiberg · 1–11 March 2027

Workshop on Quantitative Microstructure Analysis

Two weeks at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany: concepts and tools in the first, the latest analysis methods and the impact of AI in the second. On site or online, and one week is possible too.

1–11 March 2027: week 1 from Monday 1 to Friday 5 March, week 2 from Monday 8 to Thursday 11 March. Fees and registration will be announced here. The 2027 workshop follows the format of the 2026 workshop.

New in 2027

AI-assisted analysisLet an AI assistant draft an MTEX script from a plain description, then check every line of it against the data. You learn what to ask for and how to verify the answer.
MTEX for PythonEvery exercise in MATLAB and in Python, with the same commands. The Python version needs no MATLAB licence.
Week 1

Understanding quantitative microstructure analysis

Daily lectures on the concepts behind EBSD, texture and microstructure, each followed by exercises in MTEX. You learn to

  • understand the concepts of crystallography, texture and microstructure analysis
  • visualise these concepts with MTEX
  • know which tools are available to analyse your data
  • use AI to write MTEX code reliably and efficiently
  • read MTEX code, the most compact description of an analysis
Week 2

Getting the most out of your data

A workshop on the latest analysis methods and on what AI changes in the way microstructures are analysed. Talks from every direction are welcome: metallurgy, geology, semiconductors, method development and beyond. The question that connects them is how to get the most out of the data.

  • 20-minute talks with 10 minutes of discussion
  • keynotes by invited experts, to be announced
  • discussions on new methods and the impact of AI
  • an excursion into the Freiberg silver mine
Practical information

Freiberg, Saxony

Where: TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany. How: on site or online, both weeks or one.
