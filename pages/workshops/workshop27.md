The annual MTEX workshop at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany, in **March 2027**.
Two weeks of lectures, exercises and work on your own data, on site or online.
You can also come for one week only.

<div markdown="span" class="alert alert-info" role="alert"><i class="fa fa-info-circle"></i> <b>Dates, fees and registration</b> will be announced here. The 2027 workshop follows the format of the <a href="workshop26">2026 workshop</a>.</div>

## New in 2027

**AI-assisted analysis.** Use an AI assistant to draft an MTEX script from a
plain description, then check every line of it against the data. You learn
what to ask for and how to verify the answer.

**MTEX for Python.** Every exercise in MATLAB and in Python, with the same
commands. The Python version needs no MATLAB licence.

## Week 1: Learning quantitative microstructure analysis

Daily lectures on EBSD, crystallography, texture and their link to plastic
deformation and physical properties, each followed by hands-on exercises in
MTEX:

* import, clean and segment EBSD data
* misorientations and grain boundary networks
* tensor properties: stress, strain, elasticity
* parent grain reconstruction and phase transformations
* statistics and visualisation

## Week 2: Applications, bring your own data

Researchers, PhD students and industry users present case studies and
workflows. Invited experts give introductory lectures on new analysis methods,
followed by questions and breakout sessions on the participants' own data,
from metallurgy, geology, semiconductors and beyond.

* 20-minute talks with 10 minutes of discussion
* keynotes by invited experts, to be announced
* an excursion into the Freiberg silver mine

## Practical information

**Where:** TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Saxony, Germany.

**How:** on site or online, both weeks or one.

**Fees in 2026, for orientation:** both weeks on site €400 for students and
€600 for others; online €200 and €300; the second week alone €100 and €200.
Registration was free for students of TU Freiberg and the University of Halle.
